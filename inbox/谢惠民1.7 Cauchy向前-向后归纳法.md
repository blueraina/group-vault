![[01-第一章 引论#^a356de|谢惠民数分习题第一章7题]]本文给出按题目中要求，使用向前--向后数学归纳法，的一个证明


使用Cauchy向前向后归纳法，即证明：
(1) n=1,2时成立
(2) 当$n=k$成立，有$n=2k$时成立
(3) 当$n=k+1$成立，有$n=k$成立
要证：$\huge\frac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}}{(\sum\limits_{i=1}^{n}x_{i})^{n}}\le \frac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{n}(1-x_i)}{[\sum\limits_{i=1}^{n}(1-x_{i})]^{n}}$，即证：$\huge\frac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{n}x_{i}}{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{n}(1-x_i)}\le \frac{(\sum\limits_{i=1}^{n}x_{i})^{n}}{[\sum\limits_{i=1}^{n}(1-x_{i})]^{n}}$
- 1证明：代入验证即可
- 2证明 设：$\huge \frac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{k}x_{i}}{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{k}(1-x_i)}\le \frac{(\sum\limits_{i=1}^{k}x_{i})^{k}}{[\sum\limits_{i=1}^{k}(1-x_{i})]^{k}}$，则：
$$\begin{align}
\frac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{2k}x_{i}}{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{2k}(1-x_i)}=&\frac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{k}x_{i}}{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{k}(1-x_i)}\frac{\prod\limits_{i=k+1}^{2k}x_{i}}{\prod\limits_{i=k+1}^{2k}(1-x_i)}\\
\le & \frac{(\sum\limits_{i=1}^{k}x_{i})^{k}}{[\sum\limits_{i=1}^{k}(1-x_{i})]^{k}}
\frac{(\sum\limits_{i=k+1}^{2k}x_{i})^{k}}{[\sum\limits_{i=k+1}^{2k}(1-x_{i})]^{k}}\\
=& \left(\frac{\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}}{k}}{1-\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}}{k}}\cdot
\frac{\frac{x_{k+1}+x_{k+2}+\dots+x_{2k}}{k}}{1-\frac{x_{k+1}+x_{k+2}+\dots+x_{2k}}{k}}
\right)^{k}\\
\overset{n=2的情况}\le&\left(\frac{\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}}{k}+\frac{x_{k+1}+x_{k+2}+\dots+x_{2k}}{k}}{(1-\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}}{k})+(1-\frac{x_{k+1}+x_{k+2}+\dots+x_{2k}}{k})}\right)^{2k} \\
=& \frac{(\sum\limits_{i=1}^{2k}x_{i})^{2k}}{[\sum\limits_{i=1}^{2k}(1-x_{i})]^{2k}}
\end{align}$$
- 3证明：$\forall {x_{1},x_{2},\dots,x_{k+1}}$,$\large\frac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{k+1}x_{i}}{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{k+1}(1-x_i)}\le \frac{(\sum\limits_{i=1}^{k+1}x_{i})^{k+1}}{[\sum\limits_{i=1}^{k+1}(1-x_{i})]^{k+1}}$
取$x_{k+1}\triangleq \frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}}{k}$,则：
$$\begin{align}
\frac{\sum\limits_{i=1}^{k}x_{i}}{\sum\limits_{i=1}^{k}(1-x_{i})} =&\left(\frac {x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}}{(1-x_{1})+(1-x_{2})+\dots+(1-x_{k})}\right) ^{k}\\
=& \left(\frac {x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}+\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}}{k}}{(1-x_{1})+(1-x_{2})+\dots+(1-x_{k})+(1-\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}}{k})}\right) ^{k} \\
= & \left(\frac {x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}+\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}}{k}}{(1-x_{1})+(1-x_{2})+\dots+(1-x_{k})+(1-\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}}{k})}\right) ^{k+1}\cdot
\frac{1-\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}}{k}}{\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}}{k}}
\\
\overset{n=k+1的情况}\ge & \frac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{k}x_{i}}{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{k}(1-x_i)}\cdot\frac{\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}}{k}}{1-\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}}{k}}\cdot
\frac{1-\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}}{k}}{\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}+\dots+x_{k}}{k}}\\
=& \frac{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{k}x_{i}}{\prod\limits_{i=1}^{k}(1-x_i)}
\end{align}$$
$\Box$
