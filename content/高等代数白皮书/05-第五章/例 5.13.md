> [!question] 例 5.13
> 设 $(f(x),g(x))=d(x),\ [f(x),g(x)]=h(x)$，求证：
>
> $$
>
> (f(x)^n,g(x)^n)=d(x)^n,   [f(x)^n,g(x)^n]=h(x)^n.
>
> $$

## 解答
**证明**  不妨设 $f(x),g(x)$ 都是首一多项式，$f(x)=f_1(x)d(x),\ g(x)=g_1(x)d(x)$，则 $(f_1(x),g_1(x))=1,\ h(x)=f_1(x)g_1(x)d(x)$。由[[例 5.11]] 可知，$(f_1(x)^n,g_1(x)^n)=1$，从而

$$

(f(x)^n,g(x)^n)=(f_1(x)^nd(x)^n,g_1(x)^nd(x)^n)=d(x)^n.

$$

从 $f(x)^ng(x)^n=(f(x)^n,g(x)^n)[f(x)^n,g(x)^n]=d(x)^n[f(x)^n,g(x)^n]$ 可得

$$

[f(x)^n,g(x)^n]=f_1(x)^ng_1(x)^nd(x)^n=h(x)^n.  \square

$$
