### 例 6.1

> [!question] 例 6.1
> 设 $0 \leqslant p \leqslant k - 1$ ，求
> $$
> \lim _ {n \rightarrow + \infty} \frac {\mathrm{C} _ {k n} ^ {p} + \mathrm{C} _ {k n} ^ {p + k} + \cdots + \mathrm{C} _ {k n} ^ {p + (n - 1) k}}{2 ^ {k n}}.
> $$
**解**
记 $\omega = \mathrm{e}^{\frac{2\pi\mathrm{i}}{k}}$
$$
A _ {p} (n) = \frac {1}{2 ^ {k n}} \sum_ {j = 0} ^ {n - 1} \mathrm{C} _ {k n} ^ {p + j k}, \quad \omega_ {p} = \omega^ {p}, \quad p = 0, 1, 2, \dots , k - 1.
$$
考虑 $(1+x)^{kn}$ 的二项展开式, 依次令 $x=1,\omega_{1},\cdots,\omega_{k-1}$ , 并注意到 $\omega_{p}^{k}=1\ (p=0,1,\cdots,k-1)$ 可得
$$
\left( \begin{array}{c c c c c} 1 & 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 \\ 1 & \omega_ {1} & \omega_ {1} ^ {2} & \dots & \omega_ {1} ^ {k - 1} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 1 & \omega_ {k - 1} & \omega_ {k - 1} ^ {2} & \dots & \omega_ {k - 1} ^ {k - 1} \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{c} A _ {0} (n) + \frac {1}{2 ^ {k n}} \\ A _ {1} (n) \\ \vdots \\ A _ {k - 1} (n) \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{c} 1 \\ \left(\frac {1 + \omega_ {1}}{2}\right) ^ {k n} \\ \vdots \\ \left(\frac {1 + \omega_ {k - 1}}{2}\right) ^ {k n} \end{array} \right),
$$
即
$$
\left( \begin{array}{c} A _ {0} (n) + \frac {1}{2 ^ {k n}} \\ A _ {1} (n) \\ \vdots \\ A _ {k - 1} (n) \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{c c c c c} 1 & 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 \\ 1 & \omega_ {1} & \omega_ {1} ^ {2} & \dots & \omega_ {1} ^ {k - 1} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 1 & \omega_ {k - 1} & \omega_ {k - 1} ^ {2} & \dots & \omega_ {k - 1} ^ {k - 1} \end{array} \right) ^ {- 1} \left( \begin{array}{c} 1 \\ \left(\frac {1 + \omega_ {1}}{2}\right) ^ {k n} \\ \vdots \\ \left(\frac {1 + \omega_ {k - 1}}{2}\right) ^ {k n} \end{array} \right).
$$
于是
$$
\begin{array}{l} \left( \begin{array}{c} A _ {0} \\ A _ {1} \\ \vdots \\ A _ {k - 1} \end{array} \right) := \lim _ {n \to + \infty} \left( \begin{array}{c} A _ {0} (n) + \frac {1}{2 ^ {k n}} \\ A _ {1} (n) \\ \vdots \\ A _ {k - 1} (n) \end{array} \right) \\ = \left( \begin{array}{c c c c c} 1 & 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 \\ 1 & \omega_ {1} & \omega_ {1} ^ {2} & \dots & \omega_ {1} ^ {k - 1} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 1 & \omega_ {k - 1} & \omega_ {k - 1} ^ {2} & \dots & \omega_ {k - 1} ^ {k - 1} \end{array} \right) ^ {- 1} \left( \begin{array}{c} 1 \\ 0 \\ \vdots \\ 0 \end{array} \right). \\ \end{array}
$$
注意到 $a_{0}=a_{1}=\cdots=a_{k-1}=\frac{1}{k}$ 是
$$
\left( \begin{array}{c c c c c} 1 & 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 \\ 1 & \omega_ {1} & \omega_ {1} ^ {2} & \dots & \omega_ {1} ^ {k - 1} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 1 & \omega_ {k - 1} & \omega_ {k - 1} ^ {2} & \dots & \omega_ {k - 1} ^ {k - 1} \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{c} a _ {0} \\ a _ {1} \\ \vdots \\ a _ {k - 1} \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{c} 1 \\ 0 \\ \vdots \\ 0 \end{array} \right)
$$
的解, 结合线性方程组解的唯一性即得 $A_{0}=A_{1}=\cdots=A_{k-1}=\frac{1}{k}$ . 因此,
$$
\lim _ {n \rightarrow + \infty} \frac {\mathrm{C} _ {k n} ^ {p} + \mathrm{C} _ {k n} ^ {p + k} + \cdots + \mathrm{C} _ {k n} ^ {p + (n - 1) k}}{2 ^ {k n}} = A _ {p} = \frac {1}{k}, \quad \forall p = 0, 1, \dots , k - 1.
$$

> [!question] 例 6.3
> 设 f 在 $[a,b]$ 上有 $n+1$ 阶导数 $(n\geqslant1)$ ，且 $M_{0},M_{n+1}<+\infty$ ，其中 $M_{m}=\sup_{x\in[a,b]}\left|f^{(m)}(x)\right|(m=0,1,\cdots,n+1)$ 。证明：对于 $1\leqslant m\leqslant n$ ，存在与 f 无关的常数 $C_{m}>0$ 使得对任何 $h\in\left(0,\frac{b-a}{2n}\right)$ ，成立
> $$
> M _ {m} \leqslant C _ {m} \left(\frac {M _ {0}}{h ^ {m}} + M _ {n + 1} h ^ {n + 1 - m}\right).
> $$
**证明**
对于 $x \in \left[a, \frac{a+b}{2}\right]$ 以及 $h \in \left(0, \frac{b-a}{2n}\right)$ ，成立 $x + nh \in [a, b]$ 。因此，存在 $\xi_{1}, \xi_{2}, \cdots, \xi_{n} \in [a, b]$ 使得
$$
f (x + m h) = f (x) + \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} m ^ {k} h ^ {k} f ^ {(k)} (x) + \frac {m ^ {n + 1} h ^ {n + 1} f ^ {(n + 1)} (\xi_ {m})}{(n + 1) !}, \quad m = 1, 2, \dots , n.
$$
则易见存在与 f 和 h 无关的常数 $A_{1}, A_{2}, \cdots, A_{n} > 0$ 使得
$$
\max _ {x \in [ a, \frac {a + b}{2} ]} | f ^ {(m)} (x) | h ^ {m} \leqslant A _ {m} (M _ {0} + M _ {n + 1} h ^ {n + 1}), \quad m = 1, 2, \dots , n.
$$
类似地, 存在与 f 和 h 无关的常数 $B_{1}, B_{2}, \cdots, B_{n} > 0$ 使得
$$
\max _ {x \in [ \frac {a + b}{2}, b ]} | f ^ {(m)} (x) | h ^ {m} \leqslant B _ {m} \left(M _ {0} + M _ {n + 1} h ^ {n + 1}\right), \quad m = 1, 2, \dots , n.
$$
令 $C_m = \max \{A_m, B_m\}$ , 即得要证的结论.

> 证明: 存在常数 C 使得对任何数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 及整数 $k \geqslant 2$ , 成立
> $$

$$
因此，
$$
k \sup _ {n \geqslant 1} | \Delta a _ {n} | \leqslant 2 \sup _ {n \geqslant 1} | a _ {n} | + \frac {k ^ {2}}{2} \sup _ {n \geqslant 1} | \Delta^ {2} a _ {n} |.
$$

### 例 6.5

> [!question] 例 6.5
> 证明：存在常数 $C_1, C_2$ 使得对任何数列 $\{a_n\}$ 及整数 $k \geqslant 3$ ，成立
> $$
> k \sup _ {n \geqslant 1} | \Delta a _ {n} | \leqslant C _ {1} \left(\sup _ {n \geqslant 1} | a _ {n} | + k ^ {3} \sup _ {n \geqslant 1} | \Delta^ {3} a _ {n} |\right) \tag {6.2}
> $$
> 以及
> $$
> k ^ {2} \sup _ {n \geqslant 1} | \Delta^ {2} a _ {n} | \leqslant C _ {2} \left(\sup _ {n \geqslant 1} | a _ {n} | + k ^ {3} \sup _ {n \geqslant 1} | \Delta^ {3} a _ {n} |\right). \tag {6.3}
> $$
**证明**
对于任何 $k \geqslant 3$ , 我们有
$$
a _ {n + k} - a _ {n} = k \Delta a _ {n} + \frac {k (k - 1)}{2} \Delta^ {2} a _ {n} + \sum_ {\ell = 2} ^ {k - 1} \sum_ {m = 1} ^ {\ell - 1} \sum_ {p = 0} ^ {m - 1} \Delta^ {3} a _ {n + p},
$$
$$
a _ {n + 2 k} - a _ {n} = 2 k \Delta a _ {n} + k (2 k - 1) \Delta^ {2} a _ {n} + \sum_ {\ell = 2} ^ {2 k - 1} \sum_ {m = 1} ^ {\ell - 1} \sum_ {p = 0} ^ {m - 1} \Delta^ {3} a _ {n + p}.
$$
于是
$$
\binom{k \Delta a _ {n}}{k ^ {2} \Delta^ {2} a _ {n}} = \left( \begin{array}{c c} 1 & \frac {k - 1}{2 k} \\ 2 & 2 - \frac {1}{k} \end{array} \right) ^ {- 1} \binom{a _ {n + k} - a _ {n} - \sum_ {\ell = 2} ^ {k - 1} \sum_ {m = 1} ^ {\ell - 1} \sum_ {p = 0} ^ {m - 1} \Delta^ {3} a _ {n + p}}{a _ {n + 2 k} - a _ {n} - \sum_ {\ell = 2} ^ {2 k - 1} \sum_ {m = 1} ^ {\ell - 1} \sum_ {p = 0} ^ {m - 1} \Delta^ {3} a _ {n + p}}
$$
$$
= \left( \begin{array}{c c} 2 - \frac {1}{k} & \frac {1 - k}{2 k} \\ - 2 & 1 \end{array} \right) \binom{a _ {n + k} - a _ {n} - \sum_ {\ell = 2} ^ {k - 1} \sum_ {m = 1} ^ {\ell - 1} \sum_ {p = 0} ^ {m - 1} \Delta^ {3} a _ {n + p}}{a _ {n + 2 k} - a _ {n} - \sum_ {\ell = 2} ^ {2 k - 1} \sum_ {m = 1} ^ {\ell - 1} \sum_ {p = 0} ^ {m - 1} \Delta^ {3} a _ {n + p}}.

由此立即得到
$$
k \sup _ {n \geqslant 1} | \Delta a _ {n} | \leqslant \frac {5}{2} \left(2 \sup _ {n \geqslant 1} | a _ {n} | + \frac {k ^ {3}}{6} \sup _ {n \geqslant 1} | \Delta^ {3} a _ {n} |\right)
$$
以及
$$
k ^ {2} \sup _ {n \geqslant 1} | \Delta^ {2} a _ {n} | \leqslant 3 \left(2 \sup _ {n \geqslant 1} | a _ {n} | + \frac {k ^ {3}}{6} \sup _ {n \geqslant 1} | \Delta^ {3} a _ {n} |\right).
$$

从线性方程组的角度, 容易得到插值多项式的存在唯一性条件.

### 定理 6.1

设 $k \geqslant 0, m \geqslant 1, x_{0}, x_{1}, \cdots, x_{m} \in R$ ，而 $k_{0}, k_{1}, \cdots, k_{m}$ 为非负整数， $(x_{0}, k_{0}), (x_{1}, k_{1}), \cdots, (x_{m}, k_{m})$ 两两不同。又 $y_{0}, y_{1}, \cdots, y_{m} \in R$ 。则满足
$$
P ^ {(k _ {j})} (x _ {j}) = y _ {j}, \quad j = 0, 1, \dots , m \tag {6.4}
$$
的 $n(\geqslant1)$ 次插值多项式 P 对所有 $y_{0}, y_{1}, \cdots, y_{m} \in R$ 都存在唯一的充要条件是 m = n 且
$$
\det \left( \begin{array}{c} \boldsymbol {X} ^ {(k _ {0})} (x _ {0}) \\ \boldsymbol {X} ^ {(k _ {1})} (x _ {1}) \\ \vdots \\ \boldsymbol {X} ^ {(k _ {m})} (x _ {m}) \end{array} \right) \neq 0, \tag {6.5}
$$
其中 $\pmb{X}$ 为行向量值函数
$$
\boldsymbol {X} (x) = (1, x, x ^ {2}, \dots , x ^ {n}).
$$
**证明**
记 $P(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{n}a_{j}x^{j}$ . 则有唯一的 n 阶多项式 P 满足条件 (6.4) 等价于关于 $a_{0},a_{1},\cdots,a_{n}$ 的线性方程组
$$
\left( \begin{array}{c} \boldsymbol {X} ^ {(k _ {0})} (x _ {0}) \\ \boldsymbol {X} ^ {(k _ {1})} (x _ {1}) \\ \vdots \\ \boldsymbol {X} ^ {(k _ {m})} (x _ {m}) \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{c} a _ {0} \\ a _ {1} \\ \vdots \\ a _ {n} \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{c} y _ {0} \\ y _ {1} \\ \vdots \\ y _ {m} \end{array} \right) \tag {6.6}
$$
有唯一解. 由线性代数的知识, 对任何 $y_0, y_1, \cdots, y_m \in \mathbb{R}$ , 方程组 (6.6) 都存在唯一解的充要条件是 $m = n$ 且 (6.5) 式成立.

寻找恰当的插值多项式是解决一类中值定理类问题的关键.

### 例 6.6

> [!question] 例 6.6
> 设函数 $f$ 在 $[a, b]$ 上连续可微, 在 $(a, b)$ 内有三阶导数. 证明: 存在 $\xi \in (a, b)$ , 使得
> $$
> f (b) = f (a) + \frac {1}{2} (b - a) [ f ^ {\prime} (a) + f ^ {\prime} (b) ] - \frac {1}{12} (b - a) ^ {3} f ^ {\prime \prime \prime} (\xi). \tag {6.7}
> $$
**证明**
若结论成立, 则当 $f$ 为三次多项式时也成立. 因此, 令 $P$ 为满足
$$
P (a) = f (a), P (b) = f (b), P ^ {\prime} (a) = f ^ {\prime} (a), P ^ {\prime} (b) = f ^ {\prime} (b) \tag {6.8}
$$
的三次多项式. 易见 $P$ 可由下式确定 (参见[[#^zhu-6-1|注6.1]]):
$$
\left| \begin{array}{c c c c c} P (x) & 1 & x & x ^ {2} & x ^ {3} \\ f (a) & 1 & a & a ^ {2} & a ^ {3} \\ f (b) & 1 & b & b ^ {2} & b ^ {3} \\ f ^ {\prime} (a) & 0 & 1 & 2 a & 3 a ^ {2} \\ f ^ {\prime} (b) & 0 & 1 & 2 b & 3 b ^ {2} \end{array} \right| = 0. \tag {6.9}
$$
因此
$$
\left| \begin{array}{c c c c c} P ^ {\prime \prime \prime} (x) & 0 & 0 & 0 & 6 \\ f (a) & 1 & a & a ^ {2} & a ^ {3} \\ f (b) & 1 & b & b ^ {2} & b ^ {3} \\ f ^ {\prime} (a) & 0 & 1 & 2 a & 3 a ^ {2} \\ f ^ {\prime} (b) & 0 & 1 & 2 b & 3 b ^ {2} \end{array} \right| = 0.
$$
求得
$$
P ^ {\prime \prime \prime} (x) = - \frac {12 [ f (b) - f (a) ]}{(b - a) ^ {3}} + \frac {6 [ f ^ {\prime} (a) + f ^ {\prime} (b) ]}{(b - a) ^ {2}}.
$$
事实上, 若题目的结论成立, 上一式必然成立. 令 $F = f - P$ , 则 $F$ 在 $[a, b]$ 上连续可微, 在 $(a, b)$ 内有三阶导数, 且
$$
F (a) = F ^ {\prime} (a) = F (b) = F ^ {\prime} (b) = 0.
$$
从而有 $\xi_1\in (a,b)$ 使得 $F^{\prime}(\xi_{1}) = 0.$ 于是又有 $\xi_2\in (a,\xi_1),\xi_3\in (\xi_1,b)$ 使得 $F''(\xi_2) = F''(\xi_3) = 0.$ 最后得到 $\xi \in (a,b)$ 使得 $F'''(\xi) = 0,$ 即(6.7)式成立. $\square$

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l l} P _ {4} (a) = f (a), & P _ {4} (b) = f (b), \\ P _ {4} ^ {\prime} (a) = f ^ {\prime} (a), & P _ {4} ^ {\prime} (b) = f ^ {\prime} (b), \\ & P _ {4} ^ {\prime} \left(\frac {a + b}{2}\right) = f ^ {\prime} \left(\frac {a + b}{2}\right) \end{array} \right.
$$
的四次多项式 $P_{4}$ 必然不存在或不唯一. 而另一方面, 我们知道满足
$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} P (a) = f (a), \quad P (b) = f (b), \quad P \left(\frac {a + b}{2}\right) = f \left(\frac {a + b}{2}\right), \\ P ^ {\prime} (a) = f ^ {\prime} (a), \quad P ^ {\prime} (b) = f ^ {\prime} (b), \quad P ^ {\prime} \left(\frac {a + b}{2}\right) = f ^ {\prime} \left(\frac {a + b}{2}\right) \end{array} \right. \tag {6.13}
$$
的五次多项式 $P$ 存在唯一．因此，若题目结论成立，则 $P^{(5)}$ 必然与 $f\left(\frac{a + b}{2}\right)$ 无关.

以下我们给出本题的具体解答. 令 $P$ 为满足条件 (6.13) 的五次多项式, 则 $P$ 由下式确定:
$$
\left| \begin{array}{c c c c c c c} P (x) & 1 & x - a & (x - a) ^ {2} & (x - a) ^ {3} & (x - a) ^ {4} & (x - a) ^ {5} \\ f (a) & 1 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ f (b) & 1 & b - a & (b - a) ^ {2} & (b - a) ^ {3} & (b - a) ^ {4} & (b - a) ^ {5} \\ f \left(\frac {a + b}{2}\right) & 1 & \frac {b - a}{2} & \left(\frac {b - a}{2}\right) ^ {2} & \left(\frac {b - a}{2}\right) ^ {3} & \left(\frac {b - a}{2}\right) ^ {4} & \left(\frac {b - a}{2}\right) ^ {5} \\ f ^ {\prime} (a) & 0 & 1 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ f ^ {\prime} (b) & 0 & 1 & 2 (b - a) & 3 (b - a) ^ {2} & 4 (b - a) ^ {3} & 5 (b - a) ^ {4} \\ f ^ {\prime} \left(\frac {a + b}{2}\right) & 0 & 1 & b - a & \frac {3}{4} (b - a) ^ {2} & \frac {1}{2} (b - a) ^ {3} & \frac {5}{16} (b - a) ^ {4} \end{array} \right| = 0.
$$
由此立即可得
$$
P ^ {(5)} (x) = \frac {2880 [ f (a) - f (b) ]}{(b - a) ^ {5}} + \frac {480}{(b - a) ^ {4}} \left[ f ^ {\prime} (a) + f ^ {\prime} (b) + 4 f ^ {\prime} \left(\frac {a + b}{2}\right) \right].
$$
令 $F = f - P$ ，则 $F$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上连续可导，在 $(a,b)$ 内有五阶导数，且
$$
F (a) = F (b) = F \left(\frac {a + b}{2}\right) = F ^ {\prime} (a) = F ^ {\prime} (b) = F ^ {\prime} \left(\frac {a + b}{2}\right) = 0.
$$
反复运用 Rolle 定理可得存在 $\xi \in (a, b)$ 使得 $F^{(5)}(\xi) = 0$ , 即 (6.12) 式成立. $\square$

> [!question] 例 6.9
