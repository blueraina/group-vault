# 问题 2017A03

## 依赖于
- [[例 1.14]]

## 被以下题目直接调用
- 无

> [!question] 问题 2017A03
> 求下列 $n(n \geq 2)$ 阶行列式的值:
>
> $$
> | \boldsymbol {A} | = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c c c} 1 + x ^ {2} & x & 0 & \dots & 0 & 0 \\ x + x ^ {2} & 1 + x ^ {2} & x & \dots & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & x & 1 + x ^ {2} & \dots & 0 & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \dots & 1 + x ^ {2} & x \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \dots & x & 1 + x ^ {2} \end{array} \right|.
> $$

## 解答
记 n 阶三对角行列式

$$
D _ {n} = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c c c} 1 + x ^ {2} & x & 0 & \dots & 0 & 0 \\ x & 1 + x ^ {2} & x & \dots & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & x & 1 + x ^ {2} & \dots & 0 & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \dots & 1 + x ^ {2} & x \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \dots & x & 1 + x ^ {2} \end{array} \right|,
$$

则由[[例 1.14]] 可得 $D_{n}=x^{2n}+x^{2n-2}+\cdots+x^{2}+1$ . 将原行列式 $|A|$ 的第一列拆分为 $(1+x^{2},x,0\cdots,0)'+(0,x^{2},0,\cdots,0)'$ , 于是由拆分法可得

$$
| \boldsymbol {A} | = D _ {n} - x ^ {3} D _ {n - 2} = x ^ {2 n} - x ^ {2 n - 1} + x ^ {2 n - 2} - x ^ {2 n - 3} + \dots + x ^ {4} - x ^ {3} + x ^ {2} + 1.
$$
