# 12 级高代 I 期中 03

## 依赖于
- [[例 1.21]]

## 被以下题目直接调用
- 无

> [!question] 12 级高代 I 期中 03
> 设 $n$ 阶行列式
>
> $$
> D _ {k} = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c c c c c} 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 & 0 & 1 & \dots & 1 \\ x _ {1} & x _ {2} & \dots & x _ {k - 1} & 1 & x _ {k + 1} & \dots & x _ {n} \\ x _ {1} ^ {2} & x _ {2} ^ {2} & \dots & x _ {k - 1} ^ {2} & 2 x _ {k} & x _ {k + 1} ^ {2} & \dots & x _ {n} ^ {2} \\ x _ {1} ^ {3} & x _ {2} ^ {3} & \dots & x _ {k - 1} ^ {3} & 3 x _ {k} ^ {2} & x _ {k + 1} ^ {3} & \dots & x _ {n} ^ {3} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\ x _ {1} ^ {n - 1} & x _ {2} ^ {n - 1} & \dots & x _ {k - 1} ^ {n - 1} & (n - 1) x _ {k} ^ {n - 2} & x _ {k + 1} ^ {n - 1} & \dots & x _ {n} ^ {n - 1} \end{array} \right|,   1 \leq k \leq n.
> $$
>
> 试求 $\sum_{k=1}^{n} D_k$ 的值.

## 解答
记 $D = \prod_{1 \leq i < j \leq n} (x_j - x_i)$ 为 Vandermonde 行列式的值, 则由行列式的求导公式 ([[例 1.21]]) 并经简单计算可得 $D_k = \frac{\partial D}{\partial x_k} = D \sum_{i \neq k} \frac{1}{x_k - x_i}$, 于是

$$
\sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} D _ {k} = D \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \sum_ {i \neq k} \frac {1}{x _ {k} - x _ {i}} = D \sum_ {1 \leq i <   k \leq n} \left(\frac {1}{x _ {k} - x _ {i}} + \frac {1}{x _ {i} - x _ {k}}\right) = 0.
$$
