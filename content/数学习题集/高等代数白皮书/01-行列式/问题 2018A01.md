# 问题 2018A01

## 依赖于
- [[例 1.21]]

## 被以下题目直接调用
- 无

> [!question] 问题 2018A01
> 计算下列 $n+1$ 阶行列式的值:
>
> $$
> | \boldsymbol {A} | = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c c} 0 & 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 \\ 1 & a _ {1} & a _ {2} & \dots & a _ {n} \\ - 2 & a _ {1} ^ {2} & a _ {2} ^ {2} & \dots & a _ {n} ^ {2} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ (- 1) ^ {n - 1} n & a _ {1} ^ {n} & a _ {2} ^ {n} & \dots & a _ {n} ^ {n} \end{array} \right|.
> $$

## 解答
关于 $x, a_{1}, a_{2}, \cdots, a_{n}$ 的 Vandermonde 行列式

$$
f (x) = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c c} 1 & 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 \\ x & a _ {1} & a _ {2} & \dots & a _ {n} \\ x ^ {2} & a _ {1} ^ {2} & a _ {2} ^ {2} & \dots & a _ {n} ^ {2} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ x ^ {n} & a _ {1} ^ {n} & a _ {2} ^ {n} & \dots & a _ {n} ^ {n} \end{array} \right| = \prod_ {1 \leq i <   j \leq n} (a _ {j} - a _ {i}) \prod_ {k = 1} ^ {n} (a _ {k} - x),
$$

于是

$$
f ^ {\prime} (x) = - \prod_ {1 \leq i <   j \leq n} \left(a _ {j} - a _ {i}\right) \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \left(a _ {1} - x\right) \dots \left(a _ {k - 1} - x\right) \left(a _ {k + 1} - x\right) \dots \left(a _ {n} - x\right).
$$

再由行列式的求导公式 ([[例 1.21]]) 可得

$$
| \boldsymbol {A} | = f ^ {\prime} (- 1) = - \prod_ {1 \leq i <   j \leq n} (a _ {j} - a _ {i}) \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} (a _ {1} + 1) \dots (a _ {k - 1} + 1) (a _ {k + 1} + 1) \dots (a _ {n} + 1).
$$
