# 问题 2021A02

## 依赖于
- [[例 1.32]]

## 被以下题目直接调用
- 无

> [!question] 问题 2021A02
> 求下列 n 阶行列式的值:
>
> $$
> | \boldsymbol {A} | = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c c} 1 & x _ {1} & \dots & x _ {1} ^ {n - 2} & (x _ {2} + x _ {3} + \dots + x _ {n}) ^ {n} \\ 1 & x _ {2} & \dots & x _ {2} ^ {n - 2} & (x _ {1} + x _ {3} + \dots + x _ {n}) ^ {n} \\ \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ 1 & x _ {n} & \dots & x _ {n} ^ {n - 2} & (x _ {1} + x _ {2} + \dots + x _ {n - 1}) ^ {n} \end{array} \right|.
> $$

## 解答
记 $s = x_{1} + x_{2} + \cdots + x_{n}$，则 $|A|$ 的第 $(i, n)$ 元素可展开为

$$
(x _ {1} + \dots + \widehat {x _ {i}} + \dots + x _ {n}) ^ {n} = (s - x _ {i}) ^ {n} = \sum_ {k = 0} ^ {n} (- 1) ^ {k} \mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {k} s ^ {n - k} x _ {i} ^ {k}.
$$

将 $|A|$ 的第 j 列乘以 $(-1)^{j}\mathrm{C}_{n}^{j-1}s^{n-j+1}$ 加到第 n 列上 $(j=1,\cdots,n-1)$，得到行列式的最后一列的元素为 $(-1)^{n-1}nsx_{i}^{n-1}+(-1)^{n}x_{i}^{n}$，再对最后一列进行拆分，由[[例 1.32]] 可得

$$
\begin{array}{l} | \boldsymbol {A} | = (- 1) ^ {n - 1} n s \left| \begin{array}{c c c c c} 1 & x _ {1} & \dots & x _ {1} ^ {n - 2} & x _ {1} ^ {n - 1} \\ 1 & x _ {2} & \dots & x _ {2} ^ {n - 2} & x _ {2} ^ {n - 1} \\ \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ 1 & x _ {n} & \dots & x _ {n} ^ {n - 2} & x _ {n} ^ {n - 1} \end{array} \right| + (- 1) ^ {n} \left| \begin{array}{c c c c c} 1 & x _ {1} & \dots & x _ {1} ^ {n - 2} & x _ {1} ^ {n} \\ 1 & x _ {2} & \dots & x _ {2} ^ {n - 2} & x _ {2} ^ {n} \\ \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ 1 & x _ {n} & \dots & x _ {n} ^ {n - 2} & x _ {n} ^ {n} \end{array} \right| \\ = (- 1) ^ {n - 1} n s \prod_ {1 \leq i <   j \leq n} (x _ {j} - x _ {i}) + (- 1) ^ {n} s \prod_ {1 \leq i <   j \leq n} (x _ {j} - x _ {i}) \\ = (- 1) ^ {n - 1} (n - 1) \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} x _ {i} \prod_ {1 \leq i <   j \leq n} (x _ {j} - x _ {i}). \\ \end{array}
$$
