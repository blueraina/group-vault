## 解答
**解**  将第 $i$ 列乘以 $-\dfrac{c_i}{a_i}$ 加到第一列上 $(2\leq i\leq n)$，可得

$$

\begin{aligned}
|A|
&=\left|
\begin{array}{ccccc}
a_1-\displaystyle\sum_{i=2}^n\frac{b_ic_i}{a_i} & b_2 & b_3 & \cdots & b_n\\
0 & a_2 & 0 & \cdots & 0\\
0 & 0 & a_3 & \cdots & 0\\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots &        & \vdots\\
0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & a_n
\end{array}
\right|\\
&=\left(a_1-\sum_{i=2}^n\frac{b_ic_i}{a_i}\right)a_2a_3\cdots a_n.
\end{aligned}

$$

$\square$
