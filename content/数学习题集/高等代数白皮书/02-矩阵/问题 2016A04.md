## 被以下题目直接调用

> [!question] 问题 2016A04
> 设下列矩阵 M 是可逆阵, 试求其逆阵 $M^{-1}$ :
>
> $$
> M = \left( \begin{array}{c c c c} a _ {1} ^ {2} & a _ {1} a _ {2} + 1 & \dots & a _ {1} a _ {n} + 1 \\ a _ {2} a _ {1} + 1 & a _ {2} ^ {2} & \dots & a _ {2} a _ {n} + 1 \\ \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ a _ {n} a _ {1} + 1 & a _ {n} a _ {2} + 1 & \dots & a _ {n} ^ {2} \end{array} \right).
> $$

## 解答
令 $A = -I_{n}, C = I_{2}, B' = D = \begin{pmatrix} a_{1} & a_{2} & \cdots & a_{n} \\ 1 & 1 & \cdots & 1 \end{pmatrix}$，则 $M = A + BCD$。由行列式的降阶公式可知 (参考[[例 2.67]]):

$$
| \boldsymbol {M} | = | \boldsymbol {C} | | \boldsymbol {A} | | \boldsymbol {C} ^ {- 1} + \boldsymbol {D} \boldsymbol {A} ^ {- 1} \boldsymbol {B} | = (- 1) ^ {n} \left| \boldsymbol {I} _ {2} - \left( \begin{array}{c c} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} a _ {i} ^ {2} & \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} a _ {i} \\ \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} a _ {i} & n \end{array} \right) \right|
$$

$$
= (- 1) ^ {n} \left((1 - n) (1 - \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} a _ {i} ^ {2}) - (\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} a _ {i}) ^ {2}\right) \neq 0.
$$

记 $c = (1 - n)(1 - \sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i^2) - (\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i)^2 \neq 0$, 再由[[例 2.25]] (Sherman-Morrison-Woodbury 公式) 可得

$$
\boldsymbol {M} ^ {- 1} = \boldsymbol {A} ^ {- 1} - \boldsymbol {A} ^ {- 1} \boldsymbol {B} (\boldsymbol {C} ^ {- 1} + \boldsymbol {D} \boldsymbol {A} ^ {- 1} \boldsymbol {B}) ^ {- 1} \boldsymbol {D} \boldsymbol {A} ^ {- 1}
$$

$$
= - \boldsymbol {I} _ {n} - \frac {1}{c} \left( \begin{array}{c c} a _ {1} & 1 \\ a _ {2} & 1 \\ \vdots & \vdots \\ a _ {n} & 1 \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{c c} 1 - n & \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} a _ {i} \\ \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} a _ {i} & 1 - \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} a _ {i} ^ {2} \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{c c c c} a _ {1} & a _ {2} & \dots & a _ {n} \\ 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 \end{array} \right)
$$

$$
= - \boldsymbol {I} _ {n} - \frac {1}{c} \left(a _ {i} a _ {j} (1 - n) + \left(a _ {i} + a _ {j}\right) \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} a _ {i} - \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} a _ {i} ^ {2} + 1\right) _ {n \times n}.
$$
