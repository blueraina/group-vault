## 被以下题目直接调用

> [!question] 问题 2021A04
> 求下列行列式的值:
>
> $$
> | \boldsymbol {A} | = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c c} a _ {1} ^ {2} + n - 1 & a _ {1} + a _ {2} & a _ {1} + a _ {3} & \dots & a _ {1} + a _ {n} \\ a _ {1} + a _ {2} & a _ {2} ^ {2} + 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 \\ a _ {1} + a _ {3} & 1 & a _ {3} ^ {2} + 1 & \dots & 1 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ a _ {1} + a _ {n} & 1 & 1 & \dots & a _ {n} ^ {2} + 1 \end{array} \right|.
> $$

## 解答
由矩阵乘法以及[[例 1.4]] 可得

$$
| \boldsymbol {A} | = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c c} a _ {1} & 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 \\ 1 & a _ {2} & & & \\ 1 & & a _ {3} & & \\ \vdots & & & \ddots & \\ 1 & & & & a _ {n} \end{array} \right| \left| \begin{array}{c c c c c} a _ {1} & 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 \\ 1 & a _ {2} & & & \\ 1 & & a _ {3} & & \\ \vdots & & & \ddots & \\ 1 & & & & a _ {n} \end{array} \right|
$$

$$
= \left(a _ {1} a _ {2} \dots a _ {n} - \sum_ {i = 2} ^ {n} a _ {2} \dots \widehat {a _ {i}} \dots a _ {n}\right) ^ {2}.
$$
