# 问题 2023A06

## 依赖于
- [[例 2.1]]

## 被以下题目直接调用
- 无

> [!question] 问题 2023A06
> 设数域 K 上的 n 阶方阵
>
> $$
> \boldsymbol {A} = \left( \begin{array}{c c c c} & c _ {1} & & \\ & & \ddots & \\ & & & c _ {n - 1} \\ c _ {n} & & & \end{array} \right),
> $$
>
> 证明: $\left|I_{n}+A+\cdots+A^{n-1}\right|=(1-c)^{n-1}$，其中 $c=c_{1}c_{2}\cdots c_{n}$ .

## 解答
下面给出三种证法.

证法 1 (直接计算) 设 $e_{1}, e_{2}, \cdots, e_{n}$ 为 n 维标准单位列向量, 则

$$
\boldsymbol {A} = \left(c _ {n} \boldsymbol {e} _ {n}, c _ {1} \boldsymbol {e} _ {1}, \dots , c _ {n - 1} \boldsymbol {e} _ {n - 1}\right).
$$

仿照[[例 2.1]] 的计算方法可得

$$
\begin{array}{l} \boldsymbol {A} ^ {2} = (c _ {n} \boldsymbol {A} \boldsymbol {e} _ {n}, c _ {1} \boldsymbol {A} \boldsymbol {e} _ {1}, \dots , c _ {n - 1} \boldsymbol {A} \boldsymbol {e} _ {n - 1}) = (c _ {n} c _ {n - 1} \boldsymbol {e} _ {n - 1}, c _ {1} c _ {n} \boldsymbol {e} _ {n}, \dots , c _ {n - 1} c _ {n - 2} \boldsymbol {e} _ {n - 2}), \\ \pmb {A} ^ {3} = \left(c _ {n} c _ {n - 1} \pmb {A} \pmb {e} _ {n - 1}, c _ {1} c _ {n} \pmb {A} \pmb {e} _ {n}, \dots , c _ {n - 1} c _ {n - 2} \pmb {A} \pmb {e} _ {n - 2}\right) \\ = \left(c _ {n} c _ {n - 1} c _ {n - 2} \boldsymbol {e} _ {n - 2}, c _ {1} c _ {n} c _ {n - 1} \boldsymbol {e} _ {n - 1}, \dots , c _ {n - 1} c _ {n - 2} c _ {n - 3} \boldsymbol {e} _ {n - 3}\right), \\ \end{array}
$$

$$
\dots \dots
$$

$$
\boldsymbol {A} ^ {n - 1} = \left(c _ {n} c _ {n - 1} \dots c _ {2} \boldsymbol {e} _ {2}, c _ {1} c _ {n} \dots c _ {3} \boldsymbol {e} _ {3}, \dots , c _ {n - 1} c _ {n - 2} \dots c _ {1} \boldsymbol {e} _ {1}\right),
$$

$$
\boldsymbol {A} ^ {n} = c _ {1} c _ {2} \dots c _ {n} (\boldsymbol {e} _ {1}, \boldsymbol {e} _ {2}, \dots , \boldsymbol {e} _ {n}) = c \boldsymbol {I} _ {n}.
$$

因此，

$$
\left|\boldsymbol {I} _ {n} + \boldsymbol {A} + \dots + \boldsymbol {A} ^ {n - 1}\right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c c c} 1 & c _ {1} & c _ {2} c _ {1} & \dots & c _ {n - 2} \dots c _ {1} & c _ {n - 1} \dots c _ {1} \\ c _ {n} \dots c _ {2} & 1 & c _ {2} & \dots & c _ {n - 2} \dots c _ {2} & c _ {n - 1} \dots c _ {2} \\ c _ {n} \dots c _ {3} & c _ {1} c _ {n} \dots c _ {3} & 1 & \dots & c _ {n - 2} \dots c _ {3} & c _ {n - 1} \dots c _ {3} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ c _ {n} c _ {n - 1} & c _ {1} c _ {n} c _ {n - 1} & c _ {2} c _ {1} c _ {n} c _ {n - 1} & \dots & 1 & c _ {n - 1} \\ c _ {n} & c _ {1} c _ {n} & c _ {2} c _ {1} c _ {n} & \dots & c _ {n - 2} \dots c _ {1} c _ {n} & 1 \end{array} \right|.
$$

依次将上述行列式的第 j 列乘以 $-c_{j}$ 加到第 $j+1$ 列上 $(j=n-1,\cdots,1)$，则可得

$$
\left|\boldsymbol {I} _ {n} + \boldsymbol {A} + \dots + \boldsymbol {A} ^ {n - 1}\right| = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c c} 1 & & & & \\ c _ {n} \dots c _ {2} & 1 - c & & & \\ c _ {n} \dots c _ {3} & & 1 - c & & \\ \vdots & & & \ddots & \\ c _ {n} c _ {n - 1} & & & & 1 - c \\ c _ {n} & & & & 1 - c \end{array} \right| = (1 - c) ^ {n - 1}.
$$

证法 2 (分类讨论) 由证法 1 相同的计算可得 $A^{n}=cI_{n}$ . 另一方面,

$$
| \boldsymbol {I} _ {n} - \boldsymbol {A} | = \left| \begin{array}{c c c c} 1 & - c _ {1} & & \\ & \ddots & \ddots & \\ & & 1 & - c _ {n - 1} \\ - c _ {n} & & & 1 \end{array} \right|,
$$

按第一列展开后可得 $\left|I_{n}-A\right|=1-c.$ 注意到

$$
\left(\boldsymbol {I} _ {n} - \boldsymbol {A}\right) \left(\boldsymbol {I} _ {n} + \boldsymbol {A} + \dots + \boldsymbol {A} ^ {n - 1}\right) = \boldsymbol {I} _ {n} - \boldsymbol {A} ^ {n} = (1 - c) \boldsymbol {I} _ {n},
$$

故两边同取行列式可得

$$
(1 - c) \left| \boldsymbol {I} _ {n} + \boldsymbol {A} + \dots + \boldsymbol {A} ^ {n - 1} \right| = (1 - c) ^ {n}.
$$

若 $c \neq 1$，则 $|I_{n} + A + \cdots + A^{n-1}| = (1 - c)^{n-1}$。若 c = 1，则 $I_{n} + A + \cdots + A^{n-1}$ 必为奇异阵。否则，若 $I_{n} + A + \cdots + A^{n-1}$ 为非异阵，则由 $(I_{n} - A)(I_{n} + A + \cdots + A^{n-1}) = I_{n} - A^{n} = O$ 可得 $I_{n} - A = O$，矛盾！故当 c = 1 时， $|I_{n} + A + \cdots + A^{n-1}| = 0 = (1 - c)^{n-1}$ 仍成立。

证法 3 (摄动法) 当 $c \neq 1$ 时, 由证法 2 相同的证明可得 $|I_{n} + A + \cdots + A^{n-1}| = (1 - c)^{n-1}$ . 当 c = 1 时, 令

$$
\boldsymbol {A} _ {t _ {k}} = \left( \begin{array}{c c c c} & c _ {1} & & \\ & & \ddots & \\ & & & c _ {n - 1} \\ c _ {n} t _ {k} & & & \end{array} \right),
$$

其中有理数列 $t_{k} \rightarrow 1$ 且所有的 $t_{k} \neq 1$。注意到此时 $c_{1}c_{2}\cdots c_{n}t_{k}=ct_{k}\neq1$，故由已证情形可得

$$
\left| \boldsymbol {I} _ {n} + \boldsymbol {A} _ {t _ {k}} + \dots + \boldsymbol {A} _ {t _ {k}} ^ {n - 1} \right| = (1 - c t _ {k}) ^ {n - 1}.
$$

上式两边都是关于 $t_{k}$ 的连续函数, 令 $k \to \infty$ , 取极限即得 $|I_{n} + A + \cdots + A^{n-1}| = (1 - c)^{n-1}$ 对 c = 1 的情形也成立.
