# 问题 2016A03

## 依赖于
- [[例 1.32]]

## 被以下题目直接调用
- 无

> [!question] 问题 2016A03
> 设 $\lambda_{1}, \lambda_{2}, \cdots, \lambda_{n}$ 为 n 个不同的数.
>
> (1)
> 试求下列 Vandermonde 矩阵 A 的逆阵:
>
> $$
> \boldsymbol {A} = \left( \begin{array}{c c c c c} 1 & \lambda_ {1} & \lambda_ {1} ^ {2} & \dots & \lambda_ {1} ^ {n - 1} \\ 1 & \lambda_ {2} & \lambda_ {2} ^ {2} & \dots & \lambda_ {2} ^ {n - 1} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 1 & \lambda_ {n} & \lambda_ {n} ^ {2} & \dots & \lambda_ {n} ^ {n - 1} \end{array} \right);
> $$
>
> (2)
> 设 $f(x)$ 为次数小于 $n$ 的多项式, 满足 $f(\lambda_i) = b_i (1 \leq i \leq n)$ , 请利用 (i) 的结论证明: $f(x)$ 必为如下形式的多项式 (称为 Lagrange 插值公式):
>
> $$
> f (x) = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} b _ {i} \frac {(x - \lambda_ {1}) \cdots (x - \lambda_ {i - 1}) (x - \lambda_ {i + 1}) \cdots (x - \lambda_ {n})}{(\lambda_ {i} - \lambda_ {1}) \cdots (\lambda_ {i} - \lambda_ {i - 1}) (\lambda_ {i} - \lambda_ {i + 1}) \cdots (\lambda_ {i} - \lambda_ {n})}.
> $$

## 解答
将 Vandermonde 行列式 $|A|$ 按第 i 行展开可得

$$
| \boldsymbol {A} | = A _ {i 1} + A _ {i 2} \lambda_ {i} + \dots + A _ {i n} \lambda_ {i} ^ {n - 1} = \prod_ {1 \leq i <   k \leq n} (\lambda_ {k} - \lambda_ {i}).
$$

注意到代数余子式 $A_{ij}$ 与 $\lambda_{i}$ 无关, 故上式两边都可看成是关于 $\lambda_{i}$ 的多项式. 比较等式两边 $\lambda_{i}^{j-1}$ 的系数, 可得 (也可直接由[[例 1.32]] 得到):

$$
A _ {i j} = (- 1) ^ {i + j} \sum_ {(k _ {1} <   \dots <   k _ {n - j}) \in [ 1, n ] \setminus \{i \}} \lambda_ {k _ {1}} \dots \lambda_ {k _ {n - j}} \prod_ {(l <   k) \in [ 1, n ] \setminus \{i \}} (\lambda_ {k} - \lambda_ {l}).
$$

设 $\pmb{A}^{-1} = \frac{1}{|\pmb{A}|}\pmb{A}^{*} = (b_{ij})$，则

$$
b _ {i j} = \frac {1}{| \boldsymbol {A} |} A _ {j i} = (- 1) ^ {n + i} \sum_ {(k _ {1} <   \dots <   k _ {n - i}) \in [ 1, n ] \setminus \{j \}} \lambda_ {k _ {1}} \dots \lambda_ {k _ {n - i}} \Bigg / \prod_ {k \neq j} (\lambda_ {j} - \lambda_ {k}).
$$

(2)
设 $f(x)=a_{0}+a_{1}x+\cdots+a_{n-1}x^{n-1},\boldsymbol{\alpha}=(a_{0},a_{1},\cdots,a_{n-1})',\boldsymbol{\beta}=(b_{1},b_{2},\cdots,b_{n})'$, 则由 $f(\lambda_{i})=b_{i}(1\leq i\leq n)$ 可得 $A\alpha=\beta$. 于是 $\alpha=A^{-1}\beta$ , 再由 (1) 可得

$$
a _ {i - 1} = \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} b _ {i j} b _ {j} = (- 1) ^ {n + i} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} b _ {j} \sum_ {(k _ {1} <   \dots <   k _ {n - i}) \in [ 1, n ] \setminus \{j \}} \lambda_ {k _ {1}} \dots \lambda_ {k _ {n - i}} \Bigg / \prod_ {k \neq j} (\lambda_ {j} - \lambda_ {k}).
$$

最后可得

$$
f (x) = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} a _ {i - 1} x ^ {i - 1} = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} (- 1) ^ {n + i} x ^ {i - 1} \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} b _ {j} \sum_ {(k _ {1} <   \dots <   k _ {n - i}) \in [ 1, n ] \setminus \{j \}} \lambda_ {k _ {1}} \dots \lambda_ {k _ {n - i}} \Bigg / \prod_ {k \neq j} (\lambda_ {j} - \lambda_ {k})
$$

$$
= \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} b _ {j} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} (- 1) ^ {n - i} x ^ {i - 1} \sum_ {(k _ {1} <   \dots <   k _ {n - i}) \in [ 1, n ] \setminus \{j \}} \lambda_ {k _ {1}} \dots \lambda_ {k _ {n - i}} / \prod_ {k \neq j} (\lambda_ {j} - \lambda_ {k})
$$

$$
= \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {n} b _ {j} \prod_ {k \neq j} (x - \lambda_ {k}) / \prod_ {k \neq j} (\lambda_ {j} - \lambda_ {k}).
$$
