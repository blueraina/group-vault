# 问题 2018A11

## 依赖于
- [[例 3.95]]

## 被以下题目直接调用
- 无

> [!question] 问题 2018A11
> (1) 请用相抵标准型理论证明: 若 A 为 n 阶幂等阵, 即 $A^{2} = A$ , 则 $\mathrm{tr}(A) = \mathrm{r}(A)$ .
>
> (2)
> 设 $\varphi$ 是 $n$ 维线性空间 $V$ 上的线性变换, 满足 $\varphi^{m} = I_{V}$ ($m \geq 2$), $W = \operatorname{Ker}(I_{V} - \varphi)$ . 证明: 线性变换 $\frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=0}^{m-1} \varphi^{i}$ 的迹等于 $\dim W$ .

## 解答
(1) 参考[[例 3.95]] 的推论.

(2)
设 $\psi = \frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=0}^{m-1} \varphi^i$ , 则由 $\varphi^m = I_V$ 可知 $(I_V - \varphi)\psi = 0$ , 于是 $\operatorname{Im} \psi \subseteq \operatorname{Ker}(I_V - \varphi) =$

W. 反之, 任取 $\pmb{w} \in W$ , 即 $\varphi(\pmb{w}) = \pmb{w}$ , 则 $\psi(\pmb{w}) = \frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=0}^{m-1} \varphi^i(\pmb{w}) = \pmb{w} \in \operatorname{Im} \pmb{\psi}$, 于是 $W \subseteq \operatorname{Im} \pmb{\psi}$ , 从而 $W = \operatorname{Im} \pmb{\psi}$ . 另一方面, 由 $(I_V - \varphi)\pmb{\psi} = \mathbf{0}$ 可得 $\varphi\pmb{\psi} = \pmb{\psi}$ , 从而 $\varphi^i\pmb{\psi} = \pmb{\psi}$ , 于是 $\pmb{\psi}^2 = \frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=0}^{m-1} \varphi^i\pmb{\psi} = \frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=0}^{m-1} \pmb{\psi} = \pmb{\psi}$, 即 $\pmb{\psi}$ 是幂等线性变换. 再由 (1) 可得 $\operatorname{tr}(\pmb{\psi}) = \operatorname{r}(\pmb{\psi}) = \dim \operatorname{Im} \pmb{\psi} = \dim W$.
