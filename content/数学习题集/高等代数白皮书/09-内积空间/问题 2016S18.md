# 问题 2016S18

## 依赖于
- [[问题 2016S17]]

## 被以下题目直接调用
- 无

> [!question] 问题 2016S18
> 设 A 为 n 阶实对称阵, 其特征值为 $\lambda_{1} \leq \lambda_{2} \leq \cdots \leq \lambda_{n}$ .
>
> (1)
> 设 S 为 $n \times m$ 阶实矩阵，满足 $S^{\prime}S = I_{m}$，m 阶实对称阵 $S^{\prime}AS$ 的特征值为 $\mu_{1} \leq \mu_{2} \leq \cdots \leq \mu_{m}$，证明：
>
> $$
> \lambda_ {j} \leq \mu_ {j}, \quad \lambda_ {n - j + 1} \geq \mu_ {m - j + 1} (j = 1, 2, \dots , m);
> $$
>
> (2)
> 若 $A_{m}$ 是 A 的 m 阶主子阵, 其特征值为 $\mu_{1} \leq \mu_{2} \leq \cdots \leq \mu_{m}$ , 证明:
>
> $$
> \lambda_ {j} \leq \mu_ {j}, \quad \lambda_ {n - j + 1} \geq \mu_ {m - j + 1} (j = 1, 2, \dots , m).
> $$
>
> 注本题的结论 (1) 称为“特征值隔离定理”或“Poincare 定理”, 结论 (2) 称为“Cauchy 交错定理”.

## 解答
(1) 由 $S^{\prime}S = I_{m}$ 可知 S 为列满秩阵, 故 $S : R^{m} \to R^{n}$ 是单线性映射, 从而对 $R^{m}$ 的任一 j 维子空间 $W_{j}$ , $S(W_{j})$ 也是 $R^{n}$ 的 j 维子空间. 对 $S^{\prime}AS$ 应用 [[问题 2016S17]] 的结论可得:

$$
\begin{array}{l} \mu_ {j} = \min _ {W _ {j}} \max _ {\mathbf {0} \neq \boldsymbol {x} \in W _ {j}} \frac {\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} (\boldsymbol {S} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {A} \boldsymbol {S}) \boldsymbol {x}}{\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {x}} = \min _ {W _ {j}} \max _ {\mathbf {0} \neq \boldsymbol {x} \in W _ {j}} \frac {(\boldsymbol {S x}) ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {A} (\boldsymbol {S x})}{(\boldsymbol {S x}) ^ {\prime} (\boldsymbol {S x})} \\ = \min _ {\boldsymbol {S} (W _ {j})} \max _ {\boldsymbol {0} \neq \boldsymbol {y} \in \boldsymbol {S} (W _ {j})} \frac {\boldsymbol {y} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {A} \boldsymbol {y}}{\boldsymbol {y} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {y}} \geq \min _ {V _ {j}} \max _ {\boldsymbol {0} \neq \boldsymbol {y} \in V _ {j}} \frac {\boldsymbol {y} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {A} \boldsymbol {y}}{\boldsymbol {y} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {y}} = \lambda_ {j}, \\ \end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{l} \mu_ {m - j + 1} = \max _ {W _ {j}} \min _ {\mathbf {0} \neq \boldsymbol {x} \in W _ {j}} \frac {\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} (\boldsymbol {S} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {A} \boldsymbol {S}) \boldsymbol {x}}{\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {x}} = \max _ {W _ {j}} \min _ {\mathbf {0} \neq \boldsymbol {x} \in W _ {j}} \frac {(\boldsymbol {S x}) ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {A} (\boldsymbol {S x})}{(\boldsymbol {S x}) ^ {\prime} (\boldsymbol {S x})} \\ = \max _ {\boldsymbol {S} (W _ {j})} \min _ {\boldsymbol {0} \neq \boldsymbol {y} \in \boldsymbol {S} (W _ {j})} \frac {\boldsymbol {y} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {A} \boldsymbol {y}}{\boldsymbol {y} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {y}} \leq \max _ {V _ {j}} \min _ {\boldsymbol {0} \neq \boldsymbol {y} \in V _ {j}} \frac {\boldsymbol {y} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {A} \boldsymbol {y}}{\boldsymbol {y} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {y}} = \lambda_ {n - j + 1}. \\ \end{array}
$$

(2)
设 $A_{m}$ 是由 A 的第 $i_{1}, \cdots, i_{m}$ 行和列构成的 m 阶主子阵, 取 S 为单位阵 $I_{n}$ 的第 $i_{1}, \cdots, i_{m}$ 列构成的 $n \times m$ 矩阵, 则 $S'AS = A_{m}$ , 于是 (2) 是 (1) 的推论.
