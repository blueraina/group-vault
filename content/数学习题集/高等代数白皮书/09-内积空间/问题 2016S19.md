> [!question] 问题 2016S19
> 设 n 阶实对称阵 A, B 的特征值分别为 $\lambda_{1} \leq \lambda_{2} \leq \cdots \leq \lambda_{n}, \mu_{1} \leq \mu_{2} \leq \cdots \leq \mu_{n}, C = A + B$ 的特征值为 $\nu_{1} \leq \nu_{2} \leq \cdots \leq \nu_{n}$，证明：
>
> $$
> \lambda_ {j} + \mu_ {1} \leq \nu_ {j} \leq \lambda_ {j} + \mu_ {n} (j = 1, 2, \dots , n).
> $$
>
> 特别地，
>
> $$
> \left| \nu_ {j} - \lambda_ {j} \right| \leq \| \boldsymbol {B} \| _ {2} := \max \left\{\left| \mu_ {1} \right|, \left| \mu_ {n} \right| \right\}.
> $$
>
> 注本题的结论称为“Weyl 摄动定理”.

## 解答
由 (1.5.4) 式可知, $\lambda_{j} = \max_{0 \neq x \in U_{j}} \frac{x' A x}{x' x}$ , 其中 $U_{j}$ 是 $R^{n}$ 的某个 j 维子空间. 由 [[问题 2016S17]] 的结论可得:

$$
\begin{array}{l} \nu_ {j} = \min _ {V _ {j}} \max _ {\mathbf {0} \neq \boldsymbol {x} \in V _ {j}} \frac {\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {C} \boldsymbol {x}}{\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {x}} \leq \max _ {\mathbf {0} \neq \boldsymbol {x} \in U _ {j}} \frac {\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} (\boldsymbol {A} + \boldsymbol {B}) \boldsymbol {x}}{\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {x}} \\ \leq \max _ {\mathbf {0} \neq \boldsymbol {x} \in U _ {j}} \frac {\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {A} \boldsymbol {x}}{\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {x}} + \max _ {\mathbf {0} \neq \boldsymbol {x} \in U _ {j}} \frac {\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {B} \boldsymbol {x}}{\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {x}} \leq \lambda_ {j} + \max _ {\mathbf {0} \neq \boldsymbol {x} \in \mathbb {R} ^ {n}} \frac {\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {B} \boldsymbol {x}}{\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {x}} = \lambda_ {j} + \mu_ {n}. \\ \end{array}
$$

由 (1.5.4) 式可知, $\lambda_{j} = \min_{0 \neq x \in U_{j-1}^{\perp}} \frac{x' A x}{x' x}$, 其中 $U_{j-1}^{\perp}$ 是 $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ 的某个 $n - j + 1$ 维子空间. 由 [[问题 2016S17]] 的结论可得:

$$
\begin{array}{l} \nu_ {j} = \max _ {V _ {n - j + 1}} \min _ {\mathbf {0} \neq \boldsymbol {x} \in V _ {n - j + 1}} \frac {\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {C} \boldsymbol {x}}{\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {x}} \geq \min _ {\mathbf {0} \neq \boldsymbol {x} \in U _ {j - 1} ^ {\perp}} \frac {\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} (\boldsymbol {A} + \boldsymbol {B}) \boldsymbol {x}}{\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {x}} \\ \geq \min _ {\mathbf {0} \neq \boldsymbol {x} \in U _ {j - 1} ^ {\perp}} \frac {\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {A} \boldsymbol {x}}{\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {x}} + \min _ {\mathbf {0} \neq \boldsymbol {x} \in U _ {j - 1} ^ {\perp}} \frac {\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {B} \boldsymbol {x}}{\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {x}} \geq \lambda_ {j} + \min _ {\mathbf {0} \neq \boldsymbol {x} \in \mathbb {R} ^ {n}} \frac {\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {B} \boldsymbol {x}}{\boldsymbol {x} ^ {\prime} \boldsymbol {x}} = \lambda_ {j} + \mu_ {1}. \\ \end{array}
$$

由上述两个不等式即得 $\left|\nu_{j}-\lambda_{j}\right|\leq\left\|B\right\|_{2}:=\max\left\{\left|\mu_{1}\right|,\left|\mu_{n}\right|\right\}$.

注本题是[[例 9.53]] 的推广.
